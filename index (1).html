Time Management Tips

  • Use the 25–5 Method — Study for 25 minutes and take a 5-minute break.
  • Set Daily Priorities — Decide what must be completed today.
  • Break Large Tasks — Turn a large chapter into smaller tasks.
  • Avoid Multitasking — Focus on one subject or task at a time.
  • Remove Distractions — Keep your phone away during focused study.
  • Use a Realistic Timetable — Do not schedule more study time than you can realistically complete.
  • Leave Time for Revision — Do not spend all your time learning new topics.
  • Track Your Progress — Check what you completed at the end of the day.

Exam Rescue Tips

If You Have Very Little Time

  • Prioritize important topics
  • Focus on key concepts
  • Practice past papers
  • Avoid spending too much time making perfect notes
  • Focus on what can realistically be completed

If You Feel Overwhelmed

  • Take a short break
  • Break the syllabus into smaller tasks
  • Focus on one task at a time
  • Follow your study plan
  • Avoid comparing your progress with others

For Better Memory

  • Use active recall
  • Use spaced revision
  • Explain concepts in your own words
  • Test yourself
  • Practice instead of only rereading

Before the Exam

  • Prepare required materials
  • Review key points
  • Get enough rest
  • Arrive on time
  • Read instructions carefully

"Don't panic. Prioritize. Plan. Practice."
